Add to Casey

1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

Add to Casey

2a. Many covers at once (fastest):

  1. Search on mobile.yangkeduo.com (Pinduoduo), e.g. "ins风可爱手机壳". Scroll down to load more covers.
  2. Click the Add to Casey bookmark. All covers from the search show in a grid.
  3. Tap the covers you like, then Save selected. Photos, English name, price and models are filled in automatically.

2b. One cover (to pick photos and name yourself):

  1. Open the cover's page on Pinduoduo and click Add to Casey.
  2. Tap the photos to use (one design, no text photos), check the name, click Save.

New covers show on the site after a page refresh. To fix a name, price or photo, or to delete a cover, change its row in the "Casey Products" Google Sheet.
