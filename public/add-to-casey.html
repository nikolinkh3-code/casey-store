Add to Casey

1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

Add to Casey

2. How to use it:

  1. Open a phone case on mobile.yangkeduo.com (Pinduoduo).
  2. Click the Add to Casey bookmark.
  3. Tap the photos to use (one design, no text photos), type an English name, check the models.
  4. Click Save. A small window says "Saved to Casey ✓". The product shows on the site after a page refresh.

To edit or delete a product, change its row in the "Casey Products" Google Sheet.
